General Information

SIRDO (School It Research & Development Organization) is a non-profit technology organization run by students. We focus on:

  • Providing technology education to school students
  • Conducting research and development projects
  • Organizing workshops and training programs
  • Promoting innovation and creativity in education
  • Building a community of young tech enthusiasts

Our programs are primarily designed for:

  • School students from grades 6-12
  • College students interested in technology
  • Young learners aged 12-18 years
  • Educators and parents looking to support tech education

Some advanced programs may have specific prerequisites.

Yes, SIRDO is a properly registered non-profit organization in Bangladesh. We operate with full transparency and comply with all relevant regulations for educational organizations.

Our main office is located in Rupatoli, Barishal. We operate through:

  • Physical workshops and sessions in Barishal
  • Online programs accessible nationwide
  • Collaboration with schools and educational institutions
  • Community centers for practical sessions

Programs & Projects

We offer a diverse range of programs including:

  • Coding Bootcamps: Python, JavaScript, Web Development
  • Robotics Workshops: Arduino, Raspberry Pi projects
  • Research Projects: Student-led research initiatives
  • Competition Training: Preparation for tech competitions
  • Career Guidance: Tech career counseling and mentorship
  • Community Projects: Real-world problem solving

Program durations vary based on type and intensity:

  • Short Workshops: 1-3 days
  • Basic Courses: 4-8 weeks
  • Advanced Programs: 3-6 months
  • Research Projects: 6-12 months
  • Mentorship Programs: Ongoing support

Yes, we provide certificates for successfully completed programs:

  • Digital certificates for all completed courses
  • Printed certificates available on request
  • Certificates include program details and skills acquired
  • Special achievement certificates for outstanding performance
  • Project completion certificates for research initiatives

Courses & Learning

Prerequisites vary by course level:

  • Beginner Courses: No prior experience required
  • Intermediate Courses: Basic computer skills recommended
  • Advanced Courses: Previous programming experience needed
  • All Courses: Access to a computer and internet
  • Specific Requirements: Detailed in each course description

Yes, we offer flexible learning options:

  • Online Courses: Live sessions and recorded content
  • Offline Workshops: Hands-on sessions in Barishal
  • Hybrid Programs: Combination of online and in-person
  • Self-Paced Learning: Pre-recorded materials with support
  • School Partnerships: Courses delivered at partner schools

We provide comprehensive support throughout your learning journey:

  • Dedicated instructors and mentors
  • Regular Q&A sessions and office hours
  • Online discussion forums and communities
  • Technical support for setup and issues
  • Progress tracking and feedback sessions
  • Career guidance and project assistance

Registration & Enrollment

You can register through multiple channels:

  • Online Registration: Through our website portal
  • Email Registration: Send details to official.sirdo@gmail.com
  • Phone Registration: Call +880 16023081172
  • In-Person: Visit our Barishal office
  • School Coordination: Through partner schools

Our fee structure is designed to be accessible:

  • Free Programs: Many workshops and basic courses are free
  • Paid Courses: Advanced programs have nominal fees
  • Scholarships: Available for deserving students
  • Group Discounts: Special rates for group registrations
  • Payment Plans: Flexible payment options available

Exact fees are specified in each program description.

We have a fair cancellation and refund policy:

  • Full Refund: Cancellation 7+ days before program start
  • Partial Refund: 50% refund within 7 days of start
  • No Refund: After program commencement
  • Program Transfer: Option to transfer to another batch
  • Special Circumstances: Considered case by case

Technical Requirements

Basic requirements for online participation:

  • Computer: Laptop or desktop (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
  • Internet: Stable broadband connection (minimum 2 Mbps)
  • Webcam & Microphone: For interactive sessions
  • Software: Web browser, required development tools
  • Storage: Adequate storage for projects and software

Specific requirements are provided for each course.

Most of our courses use free and open-source software:

  • Programming: Python, VS Code, Git - all free
  • Design: Figma, Canva - free versions available
  • Development: Free development tools and platforms
  • Resources: Free learning materials and documentation
  • Hardware: Basic components provided for workshops

Any paid tools will be clearly mentioned in course requirements.
