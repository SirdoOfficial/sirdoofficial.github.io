Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to School It Research & Development Organization (SIRDO). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not access our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number, address)
  • Student information (school, grade level, age)
  • Parent/guardian information for minor students
  • Educational background and interests
  • Payment information for course registrations

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referring website and search terms
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining our educational services
  • Processing course registrations and payments
  • Communicating with students and parents
  • Improving our website and services
  • Sending educational updates and newsletters
  • Ensuring the safety and security of our platform
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and providing our services, such as:

  • Payment processors
  • Email service providers
  • Cloud hosting services
  • Educational platform providers

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure servers and firewalls
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Employee training on data protection

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for school students, and we take children's privacy very seriously:

  • We collect minimal personal information from children
  • Parental consent is required for students under 13
  • Parents can review, update, or delete their child's information
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children without parental consent
  • Educational content is age-appropriate and safe

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website:

7.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing Cookies: Track effectiveness of our marketing efforts

7.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. However, disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your information
  • Portability: Receive your information in a readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Our retention periods include:

  • Student records: Until the student completes our programs or requests deletion
  • Website analytics: 26 months
  • Financial records: 7 years for tax purposes
  • Communication records: 3 years from last contact

10. International Data Transfers

As an organization based in Bangladesh, we primarily process and store data within the country. However, some of our service providers may be located outside Bangladesh. In such cases, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website
  • Sending email notifications to registered users
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
